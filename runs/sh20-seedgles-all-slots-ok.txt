Why the 6 int-ABI slots resolved now (the SH20 fix): resolve_gles_int built its
CString cache-key from the RAW name (with a trailing NUL), so elfjit's
--renderframe-seedgles (which passes format!("{name}\0")) always got None even
for whitelisted int-ABI names (glClear/glViewport/glColorMask/glDepthMask/
glStencilMask/glClearStencil) that Mesa exports. resolve_gles_mixed strips the
NUL first and worked (that's why slots 0/2 resolved), the int resolver did not.
Fixed resolve_gles_int (and the identical latent bug in resolve_egl) to build the
key from the NUL-stripped name. Regression test pins the 8 names resolve with a
trailing NUL. Workspace 471/0 (was 470).

Un-skipped graphics gate (SH20): resolve_gles_mixed_float_and_stack_abi_
execute_real_mesa formerly silently SKIPPED its whole EGL/GLES body because
every NUL-terminated resolve_egl call returned None -> `else return`. Now it
runs a real surfaceless EGL->GLES chain through the JIT bridges and passes,
including a float-bridge glClearColor -> glReadPixels real-pixel check. That
exposed and fixed 3 latent harness bugs: (1) eglChooseConfig/eglCreateContext
attrib arrays must be i32 (EGLint*), not u64; (2) surfaceless needs a bound
pbuffer surface (not EGL_NO_SURFACE) for a queryable draw buffer; (3) Mesa surfaceless
llvmpipe reports GL_INVALID_ENUM for glGetFloatv(GL_COLOR_CLEAR_VALUE), so the
state-query form was replaced with glClear+glReadPixels pixel verification.
